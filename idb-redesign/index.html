The Cradle of the Yoruba Civilization

Building the Ife of Tomorrow, Rooted in the Ife of Old

The Ife Development Board unites the descendants and friends of Ile-Ife in advancing the culture, economy, and wellbeing of the ancestral home of the Yoruba people.

Our Vision

A prosperous Ife, guided by heritage and driven by its people.

We work alongside traditional institutions, government, and the global Ife community to deliver infrastructure, preserve culture, and open economic opportunity for every ward of Ile-Ife.
