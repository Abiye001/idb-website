Discover the Places That Define Ile-Ife

From ancient shrines to modern institutions, explore the landmarks that make Ife the cradle of Yoruba civilisation.

Good to Know

Travel Tips

  • Ile-Ife is roughly a 30-minute drive from Ife International Airport and well connected by road from Ibadan and Lagos.
  • Dress modestly when visiting sacred sites or the Palace grounds.
  • Sites marked "Limited Access" require prior arrangement — contact the Board ahead of your visit.
  • The Resource Centre can arrange local guides for a more in-depth cultural tour.
